Tips & Tricks

  • Smart labels — in 2D mode, switch to "Smart" label mode to automatically hide overlapping labels while keeping the important ones visible.
  • Dark canvas — toggle the "Dark Canvas" pill in the plot toolbar for better contrast when working with light-coloured colormaps.
  • Publication style — switch to the "Publication" plot style before exporting for journal-ready formatting with serif fonts and crisp axis labels.
  • Point frequency filter — on the Statistics page, use the threshold slider in the Point Frequency section to quickly spot wells that appear in an unusually high number of rejected triangles.
  • Multiple datasets — open several files at once to compare different measurement campaigns. Each dataset has its own tab and independent settings.
  • Reset if stuck — the "Reset Application" button in the toolbar clears everything and returns to the welcome screen. Your files on disk are never modified.
